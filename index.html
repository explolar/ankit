01. About Me

I started as an agricultural engineer building IoT fertigation systems and an autonomous spraying rover, then moved into the data side of Earth science. Today I work where deep learning meets weather, water and climate: reconstructing radar reflectivity from INSAT-3DR at IITM Pune, removing clouds from LISS-IV imagery with NAFNet + diffusion for ISRO, and quantifying physical climate risk for industry at EXPERIQS.

I care about models that are evaluated honestly - spatial cross-validation, calibration, ablations, pre-registered designs - and about shipping them: FastAPI + Next.js platforms on Cloud Run, LLM tool-calling agents, HPC pipelines. My M.Tech thesis builds a 30-year WRF + ML precipitation bias-correction climatology over Bihar & Jharkhand.

Currently open to ML / GeoAI / climate-tech roles and research collaborations.
