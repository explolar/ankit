VyomJal — Climate Digital Twin

🏆 ISRO BAH 2026 Finalist

AI-powered digital twin of India’s climate (Madhya Pradesh pilot, BAH PS-5)
Fuses IMD observations, ERA5/ERA5-Land reanalysis, DEM and land cover at 0.25°; a ConvLSTM encoder–decoder learns spatio-temporal dynamics and serves 1–5 day rainfall and Tmax forecasts.
